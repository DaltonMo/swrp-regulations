## Article I — Purpose

[SERVER NAME] exists to recreate the experience of belonging to a military unit — structure, shared routine, earned responsibility, and small-unit cohesion — inside the Star Wars universe on Garry's Mod.

We are not a deathmatch server with rank tags, and we are not a punishment simulator. We are a community where the chain of command is load-bearing, where what you can do is something you earned, and where the people next to you are the reason you log in.

**Three commitments this community makes to every member:**

1. **Rank is earned, never sold.** No amount of money moves you up the chain.
2. **Authority is accountable.** Every punishment has evidence, every decision has an appeal, and no one is above the process — including the owner.
3. **Effort is recognized.** Time you put in is logged, visible, and counts toward something.

## Article II — Scope and Jurisdiction

This Charter governs conduct in all [SERVER NAME] spaces:

- The Garry's Mod game server(s), including staging and event servers
- The official Discord server, including voice channels and DMs *between members regarding server business*
- The community website, forums, and personnel records system
- Steam group, Workshop collection comments, and any official social accounts

**Off-platform conduct** is generally outside our jurisdiction, with three exceptions where we will act regardless of where it occurred:
1. Credible threats of violence against a member
2. Sexual misconduct involving a minor
3. Coordinated harassment or doxxing of a member

**Age policy:** Minimum age 15. Members under 18 are not eligible for Management or Head of Staff positions.

## Article IV — Punishment Matrix

### Tier Definitions

| Tier | Nature | First Offense | Second | Third |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Minor / correctable | Verbal warning (logged) | Formal warning | 1–3 day ban |
| **2** | Disruptive | Formal warning | 1–3 day ban | 7 day ban |
| **3** | Serious | 3–7 day ban | 14–30 day ban | Permanent |
| **4** | Severe | 30 day ban | Permanent | — |
| **5** | Zero tolerance | Permanent | — | — |

### Governing Principles

**Correction before punishment.** Tier 1 violations from a new member get an explanation first. A rule nobody explained to them is a rule we failed to teach, not a rule they broke.

**Warnings expire.** Tier 1 and 2 warnings drop off your record after **90 days** of clean conduct. Tier 3+ records are permanent but are considered in context.

**Staff may adjust one tier in either direction** with written justification in the log. Adjustments of more than one tier require Senior Administrator approval.

**Aggravating factors** (may raise a tier): premeditation, targeting a new member, abuse of a leadership or staff position, repeating the same offense after correction, doing it during a public event.

**Mitigating factors** (may lower a tier): self-reporting, genuine confusion about the rule, immediate cessation on being asked, clean record over long tenure.

**Punishments run parallel, not stacked.** A single incident produces one punishment, not one per rule technically broken.

### Non-Ban Sanctions

Available where a ban is disproportionate:

| Sanction | Use case |
|---|---|
| Voice mute (1–72h) | Comms violations |
| Text mute (1–72h) | Chat violations |
| Prop restriction | Prop abuse |
| Job/battalion restriction | Persistent fail-RP in a specific role |
| Qualification suspension | Negligence with a qualified capability (see Command Handbook §6) |
| Command probation | Leadership failure, IC misconduct by an officer |

## Article V — Appeals

### The Right of Appeal

**Every punishment is appealable.** No exceptions, including permanent bans and including punishments issued by Management.

### Process

1. **File** — Submit an appeal via the appeals form on the website or Discord. Appeals require: your Steam ID, the punishment, the issuing staff member, and your account of what happened.
2. **Assignment** — The appeal is assigned to a staff member **who was not involved in the original punishment** and who is at least equal in rank to the issuer.
3. **Review** — The reviewer examines the attached evidence, the logs, and both accounts. They may request additional statements.
4. **Ruling** — Uphold, reduce, or overturn. Ruling is delivered in writing with reasoning.
5. **Escalation** — A denied appeal may be escalated **once** to the Head of Staff, or to the Ombudsman if the Head of Staff was involved.

### Standards

- **Response SLA: 72 hours.** An appeal with no response in 72 hours is automatically escalated to the Head of Staff.
- **Evidence burden is on the server, not the accused.** If a punishment has no evidence attached to the log, the appeal is granted automatically. This rule is absolute and has no exceptions.
- **Appeals are not popularity contests.** Community sentiment, Discord petitions, and "everyone likes them" are not evidence and carry no weight.
- **Cooling-off period:** one appeal per punishment. A second appeal on the same punishment may be filed after 30 days if new evidence exists.
- **Bad-faith appeals** (abusive, spam, obviously fabricated) may be closed without review and count as a Tier 1 offense.

### The Ombudsman

One community member, appointed by Management for a 6-month term, who:
- Is **not** in the staff or command chain
- Can receive any complaint, including about Management
- Has standing to escalate directly to the Owner
- Publishes a quarterly summary of complaint volume and categories (anonymized)

This role exists so that grievances have somewhere to go other than a Discord blowup.

## Article VI — Data and Privacy

- We log gameplay actions, chat, punishments, and duty time. Logs are retained **12 months**.
- Voice is not recorded by default. Members may record their own comms for evidence; recording others is permitted only for evidence purposes and may not be published outside a report.
- We do not collect real-world personal information. Do not provide it to staff, and staff must not request it.
- Members may request deletion of their personnel record on departure. Punishment records are retained regardless, as they are the basis of ban enforcement.

## Article VII — Monetization Policy

**What may be purchased:** cosmetic customization, callsign slots, extra character slots, queue priority, and quality-of-life conveniences.

**What may never be purchased, under any circumstance:** rank, battalion membership, qualifications, staff positions, command positions, combat advantage, immunity from rules, or leniency in punishment.

All payments are **donations toward server operating costs**. Nothing sold is a Star Wars product; this is a non-commercial fan community and holds no license from Lucasfilm or Disney.

Donations do not create standing in any dispute. A donor and a non-donor receive identical treatment in a sit, an appeal, and a promotion board. Staff who give preferential treatment to donors face Tier 3 sanction.

## Article VIII — Amendment

- Amendments are proposed by any member via the suggestions channel, or by Management directly.
- Amendments require Management approval and a **7-day notice period** before taking effect, except for emergency amendments addressing an active exploit or safety issue, which take effect immediately and are reviewed within 7 days.
- All amendments are logged in a public changelog with date and reasoning.
- **Rules are not retroactive.** You cannot be punished for conduct that was permitted at the time.
