## Welcome aboard

You're a clone trooper in the Grand Army of the Republic, stationed aboard the RSD [SHIP NAME]. You have a number, not a name — **yet**. You'll get one.

Nobody expects you to know anything. The only thing you can do wrong in your first session is stay quiet when you're confused.

---

## What happens in your first fifteen minutes

| Step | What |
|---|---|
| **1. Spawn** | You arrive on a recruit shuttle. Follow the marker. |
| **2. Boot camp** | 10 minutes. Movement, weapons, comms, one range run. |
| **3. Basic Rifleman** | You earn your first qualification here. Nobody fails this course — if you struggle, the instructor works with you until you pass. |
| **4. Your sponsor** | An NCO gets assigned to you. They walk you to your battalion. **Ask them everything.** That's the job. |
| **5. Your fire team** | You're placed in a team of 3–4. These are your people. |

**If nobody has assigned you a sponsor within your first session, ask for one.** It's supposed to happen automatically and it's a failure on our end if it doesn't.

---

## The five things to learn first

**1. The radio format.**
> *"[Them], this is [me], [message], over."*

**2. Three calls that stop everything.**
`CHECK FIRE` · `MAN DOWN` · `CONTACT`

**3. Weapons Cold is the base default.** Holstered. Don't walk around the ship with a rifle out.

**4. "Sir" or "Ma'am" to officers, rank to NCOs.** Reporting sounds like: *"Sir, CT-2247 reporting as ordered."*

**5. Bearing is situational.** Formations, ops, posts, and the bridge — discipline. Barracks and mess — be as loud as you want. **Knowing which room you're in is the whole skill.**

## Your first three goals

| Goal | How |
|---|---|
| **Earn a name** | Complete Basic Rifleman + 10 hours duty time, or do something memorable in an op. Your squad usually names you, and it's usually about something embarrassing. That's tradition. |
| **Pick up a second qualification** | Combat Medic and Comms are the easiest to get and the most useful to a squad. Marksman is the most fun. |
| **Make Specialist, then Corporal** | Specialist is 10 hours and 2 quals. Corporal is 20 hours, 3 quals, and your sponsor putting your name forward. |

**Rank and qualifications are different things.** Rank is where you sit in the chain. Quals are what you're allowed to do. A Corporal with EOD can do things a Captain without it can't. **Chase quals.**

---

## Things that will trip you up

- **Don't carry gear you're not qualified for.** Picking up a sniper rifle without Marksman is an armory violation, not a shortcut.
- **Don't shoot friendlies.** It happens once and it's an accident. Twice in a session and someone will have a conversation with you.
- **Don't break an event to complain.** Note it, raise it after.
- **Don't argue in a staff sit.** Say your piece once, then appeal if you disagree. Arguing has never once worked.
- **Don't lore-argue.** If you think something's wrong, file a ruling request. Do not start a thread.

---

## Speak up

If an order seems wrong, **say so before you execute it, then execute it.**

> *"Sir, recommend we approach from the east instead — request permission to advise."*

That is not insubordination and you will not be punished for it. An officer who punishes you for that is the one out of line, and you should report it. Silence during a bad plan is how operations fail.

**Three things are never insubordination:** refusing an order that breaks the Charter, asking a clarifying question, and raising a concern before you act.

## When something goes wrong

| Problem | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| Someone's cheating, harassing, or breaking rules | **Staff** — report it, don't handle it yourself |
| An order was unfair, or a leader treated you badly | **Your chain of command** — start with your NCO |
| Your chain of command *is* the problem | **The Ombudsman** — that's exactly what the role is for |
| You got punished and it was wrong | **File an appeal.** 72-hour response. If there's no evidence in the log, you win automatically. |

**You will not be retaliated against for reporting or appealing.** That's a removable offense for staff.

---

## Life happens

**Leave of absence is free, unlimited, and requires no reason.** Post it and go. School, work, family, burnout — all fine. LOA doesn't count against your activity or your standing.

The people who burn out are the ones who felt they couldn't step away. Don't be one.

---

## The short version

> Ask questions. Nobody minds.
> Learn the radio format before anything else.
> Chase qualifications, not rank.
> Take care of your medic.
> Loud in the barracks, quiet in formation.
