## 0. The System

**Rank tells you where you sit in the chain. Qualifications tell you what you can do.**

Separating these is the core progression design of this server. You do not unlock a heavy weapon by being promoted; you unlock it by proving you can use it. A Corporal with EOD can do things a Captain without it cannot.

### Universal Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Instructor required** | All certification is delivered by a holder of the Instructor qualification |
| **Practical required** | Every qual has a hands-on component. No qual is awarded on a written test alone. |
| **Logged** | Certifications post to the personnel file with date, instructor, and score |
| **Revocable** | Negligence or misuse suspends the qual pending recertification (Command Handbook §6.2) |
| **Currency** | Flight and EOD require currency (see individual syllabi). Others do not expire. |
| **Retry cooldown** | 3 days. No lifetime bars. |
| **Feedback mandatory** | Pass or fail, the candidate gets specific written feedback |
| **Duration cap** | No qualification course exceeds 45 minutes |

### Qualification Index

| # | Qualification | Prerequisite | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic Rifleman | None | Entry |
| 2 | Marksman | Basic Rifleman | Moderate |

# QUAL 01 — BASIC RIFLEMAN

**Prerequisite:** None — this is boot camp
**Duration:** 25 minutes
**Instructor:** Any Instructor-qualified NCO
**Unlocks:** DC-15A rifle, DC-15S carbine, DC-17 pistol, deployment eligibility

### Objectives
Every trooper leaves this course able to move, shoot, communicate, and report without needing to be told how.

### Course Outline
| Block | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Orientation | 5 min | Chain of command, ranks, courtesies, salute, reporting procedure |
| Weapons handling | 5 min | Draw, holster, reload, safe direction, weapons states (Cold/Tight/Free) |
| Movement | 5 min | Cover vs. concealment, bounding, stacking on a door |
| Comms | 5 min | Call format, five key prowords, "man down," "contact," "check fire" |
| Live range | 5 min | 10 targets at close and medium range |

### Standards — must pass all
- Correct salute and reporting statement
- Demonstrate all three weapons states on command
- Correctly transmit one call in standard format
- 6 of 10 targets on the range
- Zero friendly-fire incidents during the course

### Failure
Immediate retry permitted, same session. **Nobody leaves boot camp unqualified.** If a candidate can't pass in two attempts, the instructor works with them individually. This is the one course we do not gate.

# QUAL 02 — MARKSMAN

**Prerequisite:** Basic Rifleman
**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Unlocks:** DC-15x sniper rifle, scoped optics, designated marksman role

### Objectives
Precision fire at range with positive identification, target discrimination, and useful reporting from an overwatch position.

### Course Outline
| Block | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Fundamentals | 5 min | Positions, breathing, trigger control, holdover |
| Optics | 5 min | Zeroing, range estimation, magnification discipline |
| Position selection | 5 min | Cover, concealment, fields of fire, displacement |
| Observation | 5 min | SPOTREP format from an overwatch position |
| Qualification course | 10 min | Scored live-fire |

### Standards
| Stage | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Static, long range | 8/10 |
| Moving targets | 5/8 |
| Mixed hostile/friendly discrimination | 10/10 hostiles, 0 friendlies |
| SPOTREP | One correct transmission |

**Any friendly hit is an automatic fail.** No partial credit. A marksman who cannot discriminate is more dangerous than no marksman.

### Revocation
Two friendly-fire incidents in operations within 30 days = automatic suspension pending recert.

# QUAL 03 — COMBAT MEDIC

**Prerequisite:** Basic Rifleman
**Duration:** 35 minutes
**Unlocks:** Bacta injectors, revive capability, triage tools, medbay post eligibility

### Objectives
Treat casualties under fire without becoming one, and manage a casualty collection point.

### Course Outline
| Block | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Triage | 5 min | Priority order: immediate → serious → walking wounded |
| Care under fire | 10 min | Suppress or break contact first, drag technique, casualty positioning |
| Treatment | 5 min | Bacta application, stabilization, revive procedure |
| CCP management | 5 min | Establishing and running a casualty collection point |
| CASREP | 5 min | Report format and transmission |
| Practical scenario | 5 min | Three casualties under fire, scored |

### Standards
- Correctly triage 3 casualties in priority order
- Treat under fire without taking a casualty yourself
- One correct CASREP transmission
- Establish a CCP in a defensible position
- **Practical scenario: all three casualties recovered, medic survives**

### Notes for Instructors
Emphasize the discipline that saves medics: **the area gets secured first, every time.** A medic who runs into open fire is a second casualty and a squad without medical support. Teach them to say "suppress that window" before they move.

### Revocation
Repeated abandonment of casualties in operations, or persistent failure to secure before treating.

# QUAL 04 — DEMOLITIONS / EOD

**Prerequisite:** Basic Rifleman, rank of Corporal
**Duration:** 40 minutes
**Unlocks:** Thermal detonators, breaching charges, mine clearing tools, demolition role
**Currency:** Requires one refresher every 90 days

### Objectives
Employ explosives safely in proximity to friendly forces, and render safe emplaced devices.

### Course Outline
| Block | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Safety | 10 min | Minimum safe distance, danger close, "frag out," blast radius awareness |
| Breaching | 10 min | Charge placement on doors and walls, stack positioning, initiation sequence |
| Demolition | 5 min | Objective destruction, timing, sequencing |
| EOD | 10 min | Identification, cordon, render safe procedure |
| Practical | 5 min | Scored breach and render-safe |

### Standards
| Requirement | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Safety brief before every charge | 100% |
| "Frag out" called before every throw | 100% |
| Friendly casualties from your ordnance | **Zero — automatic fail** |
| Successful breach | 3/3 |
| Render safe | 2/2 |

### Revocation
**One friendly casualty from your ordnance in an operation suspends this qualification immediately**, pending investigation and full recertification. This is the strictest revocation standard on the server, and deliberately so.

# QUAL 05 — HEAVY WEAPONS

**Prerequisite:** Basic Rifleman, 15 hours duty time
**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Unlocks:** Z-6 rotary cannon, RPS-6 launcher, heavy gunner role

### Objectives
Deliver and sustain suppressive fire, and employ anti-armor weapons without endangering friendlies.

### Course Outline
| Block | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Suppression theory | 5 min | What suppression is for, sustained vs. cyclic rates |
| Position | 5 min | Emplacement, fields of fire, cover, displacement timing |
| Anti-armor | 10 min | Backblast area, target selection, engagement ranges |
| Coordination | 5 min | Working with maneuver elements, lifting and shifting fire |
| Practical | 5 min | Scored suppression and anti-armor engagement |

### Standards
- Sustain suppression on a position for 30 seconds without overheating
- Lift fire correctly on command as friendlies advance
- Destroy 2/2 armor targets
- **Backblast area clear on every launch — a friendly in backblast is an automatic fail**
- Displace after firing from a fixed position

### Notes for Instructors
The common failure is treating the Z-6 as a big rifle. Teach that suppression is a *support* function — the gunner's job is to make the enemy keep their heads down so someone else can move. Score coordination, not kills.

# QUAL 06 — COMMS / SENSOR OPERATOR

**Prerequisite:** Basic Rifleman
**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Unlocks:** Long-range comms equipment, sensor console, campaign map access, bridge post eligibility

### Objectives
Run a net cleanly, relay accurately, and translate sensor data into usable information for command.

### Course Outline
| Block | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Net structure | 5 min | Command / battalion / squad / emergency nets, when to use each |
| Procedure | 10 min | Full proword and brevity set, priority traffic, net discipline calls |
| Reports | 5 min | SITREP, SPOTREP, CASREP formats — writing and receiving |
| Sensors | 5 min | Contact classification, plotting, reporting to command |
| Practical | 5 min | Run a net during a scripted multi-element scenario |

### Standards
- 100% correct proword usage during the practical
- Correctly receive, record, and relay three reports without error
- Correctly prioritize when two elements transmit simultaneously
- Call "ALL STATIONS, NET DISCIPLINE" appropriately when the net degrades
- Plot and report three sensor contacts accurately

### Notes for Instructors
This is the most undervalued qualification on the server and one of the most important. A good comms operator makes a mediocre operation feel professional. Recruit deliberately for it — the people who are good at it are often not the people who volunteer.

# QUAL 07 — ARMOR CREW

**Prerequisite:** Basic Rifleman, 20 hours duty time
**Duration:** 35 minutes
**Unlocks:** AT-RT operation, AT-TE crew positions, BARC speeder

### Objectives
Operate ground vehicles in coordination with dismounted infantry, without running them over.

### Course Outline
| Block | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Familiarization | 5 min | Controls, crew positions, capabilities and limits |
| Driving | 10 min | Terrain handling, obstacle negotiation, hull-down positioning |
| Gunnery | 10 min | Target engagement, traverse, coordination with the driver |
| Infantry coordination | 5 min | Tank-infantry comms, dismount safety, sector responsibility |
| Practical | 5 min | Scored course with dismounts present |

### Standards
- Complete the driving course without disabling the vehicle
- 6/8 gunnery targets
- **Zero friendly casualties from movement or fire — automatic fail**
- Maintain comms with a dismounted element throughout
- Correctly execute a hull-down position

### Notes for Instructors
The dominant failure mode is armor outrunning its infantry and dying alone. Score the practical on *whether the dismounts survived*, not on how fast the vehicle finished.

# QUAL 08 — FLIGHT: STARFIGHTER

**Prerequisite:** Basic Rifleman, 25 hours duty time
**Duration:** 45 minutes (may be split across two sessions)
**Unlocks:** ARC-170, V-19 Torrent
**Currency:** 3 sim deck flight hours per 30 days, or qualification goes inactive

### Objectives
Launch, navigate, engage, and recover a starfighter without losing the airframe.

### Course Outline
| Block | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Ground school | 10 min | Airframe, controls, flight envelope, hangar procedure |
| Launch & recovery | 10 min | Launch sequence, pattern, approach, landing — **the hard part** |
| Navigation | 5 min | Waypoints, formation flying, wingman position |
| Combat maneuvering | 10 min | Engagement geometry, defensive maneuvers, gun employment |
| Check ride | 10 min | Full profile, scored by a qualified instructor pilot |

### Check Ride Standards
| Stage | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Launch | Correct sequence, comms with deck |
| Formation | Hold wingman position for 60 seconds |
| Engagement | 3/5 targets destroyed |
| Defensive | Survive 60 seconds against an instructor |
| **Recovery** | **Land intact — automatic fail if the airframe is lost** |

**Landing is the qualification.** Anyone can fly. Bringing it home is the skill, and it's what separates a pilot from a very expensive one-time asset.

### Prerequisites Note
Sim deck flight hours count toward the 25-hour requirement. Encourage candidates to log sim time before attempting the check ride — a first-attempt failure is common and shouldn't discourage anyone.

# QUAL 09 — FLIGHT: TRANSPORT

**Prerequisite:** Starfighter qualification, or 40 hours duty time
**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Unlocks:** LAAT/i gunship, troop insertion and extraction role
**Currency:** 3 sim deck flight hours per 30 days

### Objectives
Insert and extract troops under fire, on time, at the right coordinates, without killing your passengers.

### Course Outline
| Block | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Airframe | 5 min | LAAT capabilities, load limits, door gunner coordination |
| Insertion | 15 min | LZ selection, approach under fire, hot vs. cold LZ procedure |
| Extraction | 10 min | Emergency extraction, casualty loading, "popsicle" response |
| Coordination | 10 min | Working with the ground element, timing, comms |
| Check ride | 5 min | Full insert-extract profile with live passengers |

### Check Ride Standards
- Insert 6 passengers at the correct LZ within 60 seconds of the briefed time
- **Zero passenger casualties on insertion or extraction**
- Execute a hot-LZ extraction under fire
- Maintain comms with the ground element throughout
- Return the airframe intact

### Notes for Instructors
Transport pilots have the least glory and the most responsibility on the server — everyone else's operation depends on them being where they said they'd be, when they said they'd be there. **Recognize them accordingly.** A reliable LAAT pilot is worth three fighter aces.

# QUAL 10 — INSTRUCTOR

**Prerequisite:** Two qualifications held, rank of Sergeant, officer nomination
**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Unlocks:** Authority to certify others in qualifications you personally hold

### Why This Is the Keystone

**This qualification is the one that lets the server survive its own growth.**

Once players can certify other players, the training pipeline stops depending on staff availability. A server where only three people can run courses is a server with a three-person bottleneck on all progression. Build the instructor corps early and aggressively.

### Objectives
Deliver a qualification course to standard, assess fairly against a published rubric, and give feedback a person can actually act on.

### Course Outline
| Block | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Instructional method | 10 min | Explain → demonstrate → practice → assess. Why lecture alone fails. |
| Rubric discipline | 5 min | Scoring to the published standard, not to your personal preference |
| Feedback delivery | 10 min | Specific, behavioral, actionable. "Your muzzle drifted left on the reload" not "be better." |
| Failure handling | 5 min | Delivering a fail without discouraging a retry |
| Prohibited practices | 5 min | No hazing. No endurance filtering. No hard time gates. No favoritism. |
| **Practical: teach a block** | 10 min | Candidate teaches a 10-minute segment to a live class, scored |

### Standards
- Deliver the practical block using all four instructional phases
- Score three sample candidates against a rubric within one point of the assessor
- Deliver one pass and one fail with specific, actionable feedback
- Correctly identify prohibited practices in three scenario examples
- **Class engagement:** did the students actually learn it? Assessed by asking them.

### Instructor Code

1. **You are not the point. The student is.**
2. Teach the standard, score the standard, nothing else.
3. **Never fail someone without telling them exactly how to pass.**
4. No hazing, ever, in any form, for any reason.
5. Keep it under the time limit. Every time you run long, you make the next class smaller.
6. **If everyone fails, the course is broken — not the students.**
7. Recognize the student who improves most, not just the one who scores highest.

### Ongoing Requirement
- Deliver minimum one course per month to remain current
- Instructor performance is reviewed by student feedback, collected anonymously
- Poor instructor feedback is a coaching item first, a suspension second

## Appendix A — Certification Log Format

Every certification records:

> Candidate designation · Qualification · Date · Instructor · Score by block · Pass/Defer/Fail · Written feedback · Recertification due (if applicable)

Logs post to the personnel file and are visible to the candidate, their chain of command, and promotion boards.

## Appendix B — Suspension and Recertification

| Trigger | Result |
|---|---|
| Negligence causing friendly casualty | Immediate suspension, full recert required |
| Currency lapse (flight, EOD) | Inactive status, refresher required |
| Pattern of misuse | Suspension, command review |
| Charter violation involving the qualified capability | Suspension pending staff resolution |

**Recertification is the full course**, not a shortened version. The qualification means the same thing whether it's your first or your third.

## Appendix C — Build Priority

If you're standing up the instructor corps from nothing, certify in this order:

1. **Basic Rifleman instructors** — you need volume immediately
2. **Instructor qualification itself** — build the multiplier early
3. **Combat Medic** — highest impact on operation quality
4. **Comms** — makes everything else feel professional
5. **Marksman, Heavy Weapons** — high demand, easy to teach
6. **Flight** — high effort, gate behind sim deck hours
7. **EOD, Armor** — specialist, lower volume
