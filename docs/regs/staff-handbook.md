## 0. The Job

Staff exist for exactly three purposes:

1. **Keep players safe** from harassment, hate, and predatory behavior
2. **Keep the game fair** — no cheating, no exploiting, no griefing
3. **Keep the server running** — technical issues, stuck players, broken props

**That's it.** Staff do not run the roleplay, do not resolve in-character disputes, do not command troops, and do not use their tools to shape the story.

If you took this position for the powers, you took it for the wrong reason and it will show within a month.

---

## 1. The Firewall

**This is the most important rule in this handbook. Read it twice.**

> **Staff authority grants zero in-character authority. Command authority grants zero staff powers.**

| | Staff Rank | Command Rank |
|---|---|---|
| Handles | Rules, cheating, harassment, tech | Promotions, tryouts, IC discipline, operations |
| Grants | Ban, kick, mute, noclip, spawn | Orders, promotion authority, IC punishment |
| Does NOT grant | Any in-character standing whatsoever | Any moderation tool or authority |

**In practice:**
- A Superadmin playing a CT takes orders from a Sergeant and says "Yes, Sergeant."
- A Marshal Commander cannot ban, kick, mute, or issue a warning.
- **You may hold both.** If you do, you declare which hat you're wearing the moment it matters: *"Stepping out of character as staff for a second."*
- **You may never use staff tools in a dispute you are personally party to in character.** Call another staff member. Every time. No exceptions.

**Violating the firewall is the fastest way off the staff team.** It is a Strike-and-review offense on the first occurrence.

## 2. Rank Structure and Powers

| Rank | Tenure | Powers |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial Moderator** | 2 weeks | Kick, mute (≤24h), warn, teleport-to, noclip in sits, freeze |
| **Moderator** | — | + Ban (≤3 days), bring/return, spectate, gag |
| **Administrator** | — | + Ban (≤14 days), jail, model/job change, review Moderator actions |
| **Senior Administrator** | — | + Ban (≤30 days), handle appeals, approve tier adjustments >1, mentor Trials |
| **Head of Staff** | 6mo term | + Permanent ban, promote/demote to Administrator, final appeal authority |
| **Management** | — | + All server access, promote/demote Head of Staff, policy authority |

**Powers are ceilings, not targets.** Use the least force that solves the problem. A conversation solves more sits than a ban does.

## 3. Getting On, and Staying On

### 3.1 Application

- Minimum 2 weeks and 20 hours on the server
- Age 16+ (18+ for Senior Administrator and above)
- Clean record for 60 days
- Written application + voice interview
- **We hire for temperament, not activity hours.** The calmest person in a bad situation is the person we want.

### 3.2 Trial Period

Two weeks. Trial Moderators are **shadowed** — a Moderator or above reviews their sits.

- Weekly check-in with an assigned mentor
- All sits logged and reviewed
- **Roughly one in three trials should not pass.** This is normal and expected. Failing a trial is not a black mark and does not bar reapplication after 60 days.

**Pass criteria:** correct rule application, calm demeanor under pressure, evidence discipline, asked for help when unsure. That last one is weighted heavily — a Trial who escalates appropriately is worth more than one who guesses confidently.

### 3.3 Activity

- Minimum 6 hours/week on staff duty
- Respond to reports within a session
- **Leave of Absence is free and unlimited** — post it in the staff channel and go. LOA does not count against activity, does not affect standing, and does not require justification.

Burnout kills staff teams. Take the LOA.

### 3.4 Term Limits

- **Head of Staff: 6-month terms**, renewable once by Management review, then a mandatory 3-month step-down.
- Term limits exist so leadership can rotate without anyone having to quit angrily. Stepping down at term end is an honorable exit, not a demotion.

## 4. Evidence Standards

**The single hardest rule on this server:**

> **No evidence, no punishment. If a punishment log has no evidence attached, the appeal is granted automatically.**

There is no exception to this. Not for obvious cases. Not for repeat offenders. Not for Management.

### 4.1 What Counts

| Offense type | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Chat violation | Screenshot with timestamp and surrounding context, or log excerpt |
| Voice violation | Audio clip, or 2+ independent witness statements |
| Cheating | Video clip, minimum 30 seconds, showing the behavior |
| Exploiting | Video clip showing the exploit being used |
| RDM / griefing | Video clip or damage logs plus witness statement |
| Prop abuse | Screenshot plus prop ownership log |
| Harassment | Screenshots of the pattern, not a single message |

### 4.2 Standards

- **Context is mandatory.** A screenshot of one line without what preceded it is not evidence, it's a fragment.
- **Clips must show the behavior**, not the aftermath.
- **Witness statements are collected separately.** Do not let witnesses discuss it first.
- **"I saw it happen" from the reporting staff member is evidence**, but it must be written into the log as a statement with detail, not asserted as fact.
- **Evidence is attached to the log at the time of punishment**, not gathered later if challenged.
- **Retention: 12 months.**

### 4.3 Burden

The burden is on us, always. **Uncertain means no punishment.** A warning you're unsure about does more damage to the community than a violation you let slide — because the violation affects one incident, and an unjust warning affects that player's entire relationship with the server.

## 5. Handling a Sit

### 5.1 Procedure

1. **Acknowledge the report** — "On my way" or "Standby, in another sit." Never leave a report unacknowledged.
2. **Gather evidence *before* pulling anyone.** Check logs, spectate if needed. Do not tip off a suspected cheater by teleporting them.
3. **Pull both parties** to a sit location. Reporter and reported.
4. **Let the reporter speak first, uninterrupted.**
5. **Let the reported speak, uninterrupted.**
6. **Ask clarifying questions.** Do not lecture.
7. **Rule.** State the rule, the finding, and the punishment plainly.
8. **Log it** — rule, evidence, punishment, reasoning.
9. **Return both parties.**

### 5.2 Conduct in a Sit

**Do:**
- Stay calm regardless of how you're spoken to
- Explain the rule, not just the punishment
- Tell them how to appeal
- Thank the reporter, even if the report didn't hold up

**Don't:**
- Argue. State and move on.
- Threaten additional punishment for arguing.
- Discuss other players' punishments.
- Make jokes at anyone's expense.
- Rule on something you're not sure about — escalate instead.

### 5.3 When You're Being Abused

You are not required to absorb abuse. If a player becomes hostile:

1. Warn once: "I'm ending this sit if this continues."
2. If it continues, end the sit, issue the appropriate punishment for the conduct, and return them.
3. Log it and move on.

**You do not have to win the argument.** You are not required to make them agree with you.

## 6. Escalation

**Escalate — do not guess — when:**

- The punishment would exceed your ban limit
- The case involves another staff member
- The case involves Management or Head of Staff
- You are personally involved in the incident
- Cheating is suspected but evidence is ambiguous
- It's a Tier 4 or 5 offense
- The rule is unclear or the situation isn't covered
- You are angry

**That last one is real.** If you are angry, hand it off. There is no shame in "I'm too heated for this one, can someone take it."

**Escalation path:** Trial → Moderator → Administrator → Senior Administrator → Head of Staff → Ombudsman (for cases involving the chain itself) → Management.

---

## 7. Conflict of Interest

You must recuse yourself and call another staff member when:

- You are a party to the incident, in or out of character
- The player is in your battalion and the issue is battalion-related
- The player is a close friend or someone you have ongoing conflict with
- You issued the original punishment being appealed
- You stand to gain in character from the outcome

**Recusal is free and expected.** Nobody thinks less of you for it. The cost of not recusing is that every ruling you ever make becomes suspect.

## 9. Staff Discipline

### Two-Strike Policy

**Strike One:** Documented in the staff record. **Made known to the staff team** — not to the public, but not hidden from peers either. Corrective conversation with Head of Staff.

**Strike Two:** Removal from the staff team.

**Immediate removal**, no strikes, for:
- Firewall violation (using staff tools in a personal IC dispute)
- Punishing without evidence, twice
- Retaliation against a reporter or appellant
- Falsifying evidence or logs
- Any Charter Tier 4 or 5 violation
- Preferential treatment for payment

**Strikes expire after 6 months** of clean conduct.

**Removed staff may reapply after 90 days**, except where removal was for falsified evidence or retaliation, which are permanent bars.

### Right of Appeal

**Staff have the same appeal rights as players.** A removed staff member may appeal to the Ombudsman, who may escalate to the Owner.

---

## 10. The Ombudsman

Appointed by Management, 6-month term, explicitly **outside** the staff and command chains.

- Receives complaints about anyone, including Head of Staff and Management
- Has standing to escalate directly to the Owner
- Cannot issue punishments — the role is investigative and advisory only
- Publishes an anonymized quarterly summary: complaint volume, categories, resolution rate

**Why this exists:** every community that collapses does so because grievances had nowhere to go except a public Discord blowup. This is the pressure release valve. It costs nothing and it has saved communities.

## 11. Shift Coverage

- **Peak hours (7–11pm local) require minimum 2 staff online.**
- Post your availability in the staff channel weekly.
- **The staff channel is for staff business.** Social chat belongs elsewhere — a staff channel that becomes a clique lounge is the beginning of the end.
- Weekly staff meeting, 30 minutes, agenda published in advance, notes posted after.

## 12. The Standard

Every staff decision gets measured against one question:

> **Would this decision look defensible if it were posted publicly, with full context, tomorrow?**

If yes, act. If no, don't. If you're not sure, escalate.

That's the whole job.
