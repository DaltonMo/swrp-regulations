## RADIO — the format

> **"[THEM], THIS IS [ME], [MESSAGE], OVER."**

After contact is made, shorten to: *"Two-One, moving."*

| Proword | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Over** | Done, respond |
| **Out** | Done, no response needed |
| **Roger** | Received and understood |
| **Wilco** | Received, understood, will comply |
| **Say again** | Repeat your last *(never say "repeat")* |
| **Standby** | Wait, I'm busy |

**Priority traffic — interrupts anything:**
`CHECK FIRE` · `MAN DOWN` · `CONTACT`

**Common calls:** Contact · Engaging · Moving · Set · Clear · Frag out · Reloading · Falling back · Winchester *(out of ammo)* · Popsicle *(emergency extract)*

**Push to talk. Compose, then key. Wait for "over."**

## CHAIN OF COMMAND

**Enlisted:** CT → CT-S → CPL → SGT → SSGT → WO
**Officer:** 2LT → 1LT → CPT → MAJ → LTC → COL → CMDR → MCMDR

Officers (2LT+): **"Sir" / "Ma'am."** NCOs: by rank — *"Sergeant."*
Reporting: **"Sir, CT-2247 reporting as ordered."**
Acknowledging: **"Yes, sir." / "Copy." / "Wilco."** — not "ok" or "yeah."

**Disagree? Say so before you execute, then execute.**
*"Sir, recommend we approach from the east — request permission to advise."*
That is not insubordination. Silence during a bad plan is the failure.

## CASUALTY — four steps, in order

1. **Call it** — *"Man down, [callsign], [location]"*
2. **Secure the area** — suppress or break contact **first**. A dead medic is two casualties.
3. **Move to the CCP** — don't treat in the open
4. **Stay down** until treated or the timer expires — don't sprint back in

---

## ENGAGEMENT

1. Positive ID before you fire
2. Check your background
3. **"Check fire" stops everything, instantly, no exceptions**
4. Friendly fire is reportable — every time
5. Surrendered organics are prisoners; don't harm them
6. Don't engage outside your sector without telling your neighbors

## THE RULES THAT GET PEOPLE BANNED

1. No slurs or hate speech — no context makes it okay
2. No harassment, no doxxing
3. No sexual content
4. No cheating, scripting, or exploiting — **report bugs instead, it's rewarded**
5. No RDM — violence on friendlies needs IC justification
6. No metagaming — Discord and spectator info stay out of character
7. No prop abuse, no out-of-bounds
8. No mic spam or music on comms
9. Comply in a sit, argue in the appeal
10. Don't disconnect to dodge a sit

**Every punishment has evidence attached. Every punishment is appealable. If there's no evidence in the log, your appeal is granted automatically.**

## FORMATION

**Ten minutes. Fifteen is the ceiling.** Longer than that is a leadership failure, not your problem to endure.

Attention = silent, still, eyes forward. Questions at the end. Going AFK? Say so in OOC *before* it starts.

## GETTING SOMEWHERE

**Rank** is where you sit. **Quals** are what you can do. They're separate — chase quals.

Basic Rifleman → then pick: Marksman · Combat Medic · Heavy Weapons · Comms · EOD · Armor · Flight

**Instructor** is the one that matters most. Once you hold it, you can certify others.
