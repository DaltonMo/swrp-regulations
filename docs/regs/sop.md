## 0. How To Use This Document

This is the practical manual. Formations, comms, engagement, casualties, posts. It is written to be **learnable in one sitting and referenced in ten seconds.**

Section 3 (Communications) is the single highest-value page in this entire document set. If a new trooper reads exactly one thing, make it that.

**Standard of enforcement:** teach once, correct once, enforce on the third. Nobody gets punished for a procedure nobody taught them.

## 1. Bearing and Courtesy

### 1.1 When Bearing Is Required

**In effect:** formations, briefings, operations, training, at posts, in the presence of an officer, on the bridge, in the brig.

**Not in effect:** barracks, mess, sim deck between runs, off-duty hours, Discord social channels.

The barracks should be loud. That is not a failure of discipline — it is the actual thing we are recreating. Discipline is knowing which room you're in.

### 1.2 Courtesies

- **"Sir" / "Ma'am"** to commissioned officers (2LT and above). Non-commissioned officers are addressed by rank: "Sergeant," "Corporal."
- **Salute** on first meeting an officer each session, on reporting, on being dismissed. Not required in a combat zone, in the field, or when your hands are full.
- **Stand at attention** when an officer of Captain or above enters a formation.
- **Reporting:** "Sir, CT-2247 reporting as ordered."
- **Acknowledgment:** "Yes, sir," "Understood," "Copy," "Wilco." Not "ok," "sure," "yeah."

### 1.3 Positions

| Command | Position |
|---|---|
| **Attention** | Feet together, arms at sides, eyes forward, no movement, no talking |
| **Parade Rest** | Feet apart, hands behind back, eyes forward, no talking |
| **At Ease** | May move and speak quietly, one foot remains planted |
| **Fall Out** | Formation dismissed |

## 2. Formations

### 2.1 The Ten-Minute Rule

**A formation runs ten minutes. Fifteen is the hard ceiling.**

A formation that runs long is a leadership failure and will be treated as one. If you cannot deliver it in ten minutes, you are giving a briefing instead — call it that, let people sit, and stop making them stand at attention through it.

Nothing kills a server faster than the forty-five-minute formation.

### 2.2 Formation Types

| Type | Purpose | Duration | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Roll Call** | Accountability, quick assignments | 5 min | Start of session |
| **Battalion Formation** | Unit-level announcements, promotions | 10 min | Weekly |
| **Operation Formation** | Pre-op assembly, immediately precedes briefing | 5 min | Before each op |
| **Ceremonial** | Promotions, awards, memorials | 15 min | As required |
| **Emergency Muster** | Base alert, boarding, immediate response | 2 min | As required |

### 2.3 Standard Sequence

1. **Call** — "[UNIT], FALL IN" over comms and in chat
2. **Assembly** — 60 seconds to arrive; latecomers fall in at the rear without disruption
3. **Attention** — "[UNIT], ATTENTION"
4. **Accountability** — count off or roll call
5. **Business** — announcements, promotions, assignments. This is the ten-minute window.
6. **Questions** — "Any questions?" and mean it
7. **Dismissal** — "[UNIT], FALL OUT"

### 2.4 Formation Discipline

- Arrive on time. Late without notice is a counseling item after the second occurrence.
- No talking at attention. Questions go at the end.
- No leaving formation without permission.
- If you must go AFK, say so in OOC chat before the formation starts.
- **Leadership does not use formations for punishment.** Mass corrective action for one person's mistake is banned. It builds resentment and it drives people off the server.

## 3. Communications

**This is the section that matters most. Learn it.**

### 3.1 Net Structure

| Net | Users | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Command Net** | Officers, battalion leads, Naval | Coordination between units |
| **Battalion Net** | All members of a battalion | Unit-level traffic |
| **Squad Net** | 3–6 troopers | Fire team traffic |
| **Emergency Net** | All | Life-safety only |
| **Local Voice** | Proximity | Everything not requiring radio |

**Use the lowest net that solves the problem.** Squad chatter does not go on Command Net.

### 3.2 Callsign Format

`[UNIT] [ELEMENT] [POSITION]` — spoken as: "**Wolf Two-One**" = Wolfpack, 2nd squad, position 1 (squad lead).

Command elements use the unit name plus "Actual": "**Wolf Actual**" is the Wolfpack commander.

### 3.3 Standard Call Format

> **"[WHO YOU'RE CALLING], THIS IS [WHO YOU ARE], [MESSAGE], OVER."**

Example: *"Wolf Actual, this is Wolf Two-One, we're taking fire from the north ridge, requesting support, over."*

After contact is established, shorten: *"Two-One, moving."*

### 3.4 Prowords

| Proword | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Over** | I'm done, respond |
| **Out** | I'm done, no response needed |
| **Roger** | I received and understood |
| **Wilco** | Received, understood, and I will comply |
| **Say again** | Repeat your last (never say "repeat") |
| **Standby** | Wait, I'm busy |
| **Break** | Separating parts of a long message |
| **Correction** | I made an error, here's the fix |
| **How copy** | Did you receive that? |
| **Negative / Affirmative** | No / yes |
| **Interrogative** | I'm asking a question |

### 3.5 Brevity Codes

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Contact** | Enemy sighted |
| **Engaging** | I am firing |
| **Troops in contact** | Under fire, need support |
| **Man down** | Casualty, unspecified |
| **Man down, [callsign]** | Named casualty |
| **Bacta** / **Medic** | Medical assistance required |
| **Moving** | Advancing |
| **Set** | In position |
| **Clear** | Area cleared of threats |
| **Frag out** | Throwing a detonator |
| **Reloading** | Cover me |
| **Check fire** | Stop firing immediately |
| **Danger close** | Friendlies near target |
| **Falling back** | Withdrawing |
| **Winchester** | Out of ammunition |
| **Popsicle** | Emergency extraction required |

### 3.6 Discipline Rules

1. **Think before you key.** Compose the message, then transmit.
2. **Brevity is a duty.** Every second you hold the net, someone else can't report a casualty.
3. **Do not step on transmissions.** Wait for "over" or "out."
4. **Priority: emergency > command > tactical > everything else.** "Check fire" and "man down" interrupt anything.
5. **No chatter on operation nets.** Save it for local voice or squad.
6. **Push to talk. Always.**
7. **If comms discipline collapses,** the ranking element calls "**ALL STATIONS, NET DISCIPLINE**" and traffic stops until reset.

### 3.7 Report Formats

**SITREP** — Situation Report, on request or every 10 minutes during an op:
> Callsign · Location · Status (green/amber/red) · Enemy contact · Ammunition · Casualties · Intentions

**SPOTREP** — Enemy sighted:
> Callsign · Size · Activity · Location · Time · Equipment

**CASREP** — Casualty:
> Callsign · Location · Number of casualties · Severity · Extraction required Y/N

## 4. Rules of Engagement

### 4.1 Standing ROE

1. **Positive identification before engaging.** Confirm target is hostile.
2. **Fire discipline.** Aimed fire. No spray. No shooting through friendlies.
3. **Check your background.** Know what's behind your target.
4. **Cease fire immediately on "check fire."** No exceptions, no "one more shot."
5. **Friendly fire is reportable.** Once is an accident. Twice in a session is a counseling item. A pattern is a qualification suspension.
6. **Surrendered enemies are prisoners.** Droids may be disabled; organic surrenders are taken into custody.
7. **Non-combatants are not targets.** Civilians in event scenarios are protected. Engaging them is a serious ROE violation.
8. **Do not engage outside your assigned sector** without notifying adjacent elements.

### 4.2 Weapons States

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Weapons Cold** | Holstered. Base default outside alert conditions. |
| **Weapons Tight** | Drawn, fire only on positive ID and threat |
| **Weapons Free** | Fire on identified hostiles at discretion |

Weapons state is set by the ranking element and announced. **Base default is Weapons Cold.** Running around the Venator with a rifle out is a fail-RP item.

### 4.3 Alert Conditions

| Condition | Meaning | Response |
|---|---|---|
| **Condition Green** | Normal operations | Standard posts |
| **Condition Amber** | Elevated threat | All hands to posts, weapons tight |
| **Condition Red** | Under attack / boarding | Emergency muster, weapons free in contested compartments |
| **Condition Black** | Catastrophic — hull breach, abandon | Evacuation protocol |

## 5. Duty Posts

### 5.1 Post Standards

- **Report on time and in uniform.** Relieving trooper receives a brief handover: status, anything unusual, standing orders.
- **A post is not an AFK spot.** Duty credit accrues from presence *and* activity. Standing motionless for 20 minutes stops accrual.
- **Do not leave a post unrelieved.** If you must go, notify your chain and get a relief.

### 5.2 Standard Posts

| Post | Held by | Duties |
|---|---|---|
| **Hangar Deck** | Any battalion | Craft servicing, launch/recovery, deck safety |
| **Armory** | Any battalion | Issue and return of equipment, inventory logging |
| **Brig** | Coruscant Guard | Prisoner supervision, intake, release |
| **Main Gate / Airlock** | Coruscant Guard | ID verification, access control |
| **Bridge** | Naval | Sensors, navigation, comms, campaign map |
| **Medbay** | Medics | Triage, treatment, records |
| **Roving Patrol** | Any battalion | Sweep route, report anomalies |

### 5.3 Handover Brief

> "Post is [status]. Standing orders are [X]. During my watch, [events]. Nothing further." / "Post is green, nothing to report."

## 6. Casualty Procedures

1. **Call it.** "Man down, [callsign], [location]" on the appropriate net.
2. **Secure the area first.** Do not create a second casualty. Suppress or break contact before treating.
3. **Casualty collection point.** Move casualties to a designated CCP rather than treating in the open.
4. **Medic priority order:** immediate life threats → serious → walking wounded.
5. **KIA handling:** in operations, remain down until medic or extraction reaches you, or until the New Life Rule timer expires. Do not immediately respawn and sprint back into the fight — it destroys the tension and makes medics pointless.
6. **After-action:** casualties are logged in the operation AAR.

**Medic protection is everyone's job.** A squad that lets its medic get shot has failed.

---

## 7. Prisoner Handling

1. **Search** on capture. Remove weapons and equipment.
2. **Silence and segregate** — prisoners do not talk to each other during transport.
3. **Speed** — move to the brig promptly.
4. **Safeguard** — prisoners are not to be harmed, mocked, or used as roleplay props. Abuse of a prisoner is a Charter violation, not just fail-RP.
5. **Tag** — log the capture: who, where, when, by whom.
6. **Custody** passes to the Coruscant Guard at the brig. They own the prisoner from that point.
7. **Interrogation** requires an officer present and follows the Command Handbook. No torture roleplay.

## 8. Operation Procedure

### 8.1 The Five-Paragraph Order

Every operation briefing follows this structure. It takes five minutes and it makes the difference between a coordinated op and forty people wandering.

1. **Situation** — what's happening, enemy, friendly forces
2. **Mission** — one sentence: who, what, when, where, why
3. **Execution** — commander's intent, scheme of maneuver, tasks per element
4. **Sustainment** — ammunition, medical, extraction plan
5. **Command & Signal** — chain of command, net assignments, callsigns

### 8.2 Operation Flow

| Phase | Content |
|---|---|
| **Muster** | Formation, accountability, weapons/kit check |
| **Brief** | Five-paragraph order, questions |
| **Insert** | Movement to objective |
| **Actions on objective** | The mission |
| **Extract** | Withdrawal, accountability check |
| **Debrief / AAR** | Ten minutes maximum, see §8.3 |

### 8.3 After Action Review

Four questions, ten minutes, everyone participates:

1. What was supposed to happen?
2. What actually happened?
3. What went well?
4. What do we change next time?

**AAR rules:** no rank in the AAR — a CT may tell a Colonel the plan was bad. No blaming individuals. Findings go in the operation log and feed the campaign map.

An AAR where nobody criticizes anything is a wasted AAR.

## 9. Uniform and Kit Standards

- **Phase II armor is standard.** Unit markings correct for your battalion.
- **Kit must match your qualifications.** Carrying a DC-15x without Marksman qual is a fail-RP item and an armory violation.
- **Naval personnel** wear the naval uniform, not armor, except during boarding actions.
- **Helmets on** in operations, at exterior posts, and during alert conditions. Helmets optional in barracks, mess, and medbay.
- **Cosmetic customization** must remain within the Canon Scope. No armor from outside the era.
