## 0. Why This Document Exists

Lore arguments are the single most common recurring conflict in Star Wars roleplay communities, and they are almost always unwinnable — because both people are right, citing different sources.

This document ends that. **It defines what is true on this server.** Not what is true in Star Wars generally. If something isn't addressed here, §8 tells you how it gets decided.

Bring a source to a lore argument and the answer is: *"What does the Canon Scope say?"* If it says nothing, file a ruling request. Do not argue in Discord.

## 1. Era and Timeline

**Setting: 21–20 BBY, the middle years of the Clone Wars.**

Specifically: after Geonosis and the Battle of Kamino, after Phase II armor is in general issue, before Order 66. The Republic is winning on some fronts and losing on others. The war has been going long enough that veteran clones exist and long enough that the shine has worn off.

**Where our story sits:** the server operates its own front. We are not re-enacting Ryloth or Umbara beat for beat. Our campaign (see Concept Doc §7) is an original Outer Rim theater running parallel to canon events. This gives us narrative freedom without contradicting anything.

**Order 66 does not happen.** The timeline does not advance to it. If the community ever wants a server-wide finale, that is a Management decision made deliberately, not a plot twist an event planner drops on a Tuesday.

**Real-world time does not map to in-universe time.** One real month is not one in-universe month. Time passes as the campaign requires.

## 2. Source Hierarchy

When sources conflict, this is the order of authority:

1. **This document.** Always wins.
2. **Star Wars: The Clone Wars (2008–2020 series).** Our primary visual and tonal reference. If it's in the show, it's true here.
3. **The films** (Episodes II and III).
4. **Current Disney canon** — novels, comics, reference books.
5. **Legends / Expanded Universe** — permitted as a *source of flavor and detail*, particularly for unit histories, equipment variants, and planets. Not binding.
6. **Community-created lore** — our own campaign history, unit traditions, character backstories. Binding on us, invisible outside.

**Our line, stated plainly:** we are **Legends-permissive**. We draw freely from Legends when it makes for better roleplay, and we default to the show when there's a conflict. We are not canon purists and we are not running a Legends-only server. This is a deliberate choice; it is not up for debate.

**Explicitly not sources:** video game mechanics (Battlefront balance is not lore), fan films, other servers' rules, and "I saw it on a wiki once."

## 4. Clone Identity and Naming

### 4.1 The Progression

This is one of the most important pieces of roleplay design on the server. **Individuality is earned.**

| Stage | Designation | How you get it |
|---|---|---|
| **Recruit / CT** | `CT-####` (randomly assigned) | Joining |
| **Named Trooper** | Chosen name, e.g. `CT-2247 "Hatch"` | Complete Basic Rifleman + 10 hours duty time, or earn it in an operation |
| **Callsign** | Squad callsign, e.g. `WOLF 3` | Assignment to a fire team |
| **Personalized armor** | Custom paint/markings | Rank of Corporal or above, or purchased cosmetic |

**Names must be earned in-character.** The traditional route is that a squad gives you one, usually derived from something you did — good or embarrassing. Command may also grant one for performance. You cannot simply pick one on day one.

### 4.2 Naming Standards

**Permitted:** short nicknames, trade-derived names, incident-derived names, trait-derived names. Examples of the register we want: Hatch, Sparks, Crosswire, Ridge, Tally, Dust, Kettle, Nine.

**Not permitted:** real-world celebrity names, meme names, names from other franchises, offensive names, names identical to major canon characters (no second Rex, Cody, Fives, Echo, Wolffe, Hardcase, Jesse, Kix, Waxer, Boil).

**Jedi and Naval** use ordinary Star Wars-style names, not clone designations.

### 4.3 Reserved Characters

Named canon characters — Rex, Cody, Wolffe, Anakin, Obi-Wan, Ahsoka, etc. — are **not playable**. They may appear as staff-controlled NPCs in scripted events only, and only with Management approval.

This is non-negotiable. Playable canon characters wreck internal hierarchy instantly, because nobody outranks Anakin Skywalker.

## 5. Jedi Policy

Jedi are the most-abused role in every SWRP community. The policy here is deliberately restrictive.

- **Hard cap: 8% of the active roster**, rounded down, with an absolute ceiling of 10 slots.
- **Application-only.** Written application plus interview with the Jedi Council roles. No tryouts, no first-come.
- **Ranks:** Youngling (probationary) → Padawan → Knight → Master → Council Member. Council is capped at 3.
- **No Jedi Generals at launch.** The GAR command structure runs the war. A Jedi General is a campaign unlock, not a starting position.
- **Mechanically support-oriented.** Force abilities on this server emphasize saber defense, deflection, push/pull, and battlefield utility. Jedi are durable and useful, not one-man armies. Damage output is deliberately modest.
- **Force powers permitted:** saber combat and deflection, Force push/pull, Force jump, Force speed (brief), Force sense, mind trick (on NPCs only, never on player characters), Force healing (self, slow).
- **Force powers not permitted:** lightning, choke, drain, precognitive combat awareness that overrides other players' actions, and any power that removes another player's control of their character.
- **Chain of command:** Jedi advise; they do not command GAR units outside of specifically assigned operations. A Jedi Knight cannot order a Sergeant to do their laundry.

## 6. Equipment Scope

### 6.1 Armor

**Phase II is standard issue.** Phase I appears only on long-service veterans as a personal choice, and requires a rank of Sergeant or above plus a written justification. Mixed Phase I/II kit is permitted for veterans.

**Permitted attachments:** kama, pauldron, macrobinoculars, rangefinder (specialist roles), antenna (comms), visor tint variation, unit markings, personal markings.

**Not permitted:** Mandalorian armor, jetpacks outside of specific qualified roles, ARC trooper kit without ARC qualification, Commando armor (locked unit), and any armor from outside the era.

### 6.2 Weapons

Standard armory: DC-15A blaster rifle, DC-15S carbine, DC-17 pistol, DC-15x sniper (Marksman qual), Z-6 rotary cannon (Heavy Weapons qual), RPS-6 launcher (Heavy Weapons qual), thermal detonators (EOD or Assault role), vibroblade.

**Dual DC-17s** are ARC-only. **Lightsabers** are Jedi-only. **Anything not on the armory list requires a Lore Officer ruling.**

### 6.3 Vehicles

LAAT/i gunship, ARC-170, V-19 Torrent, BARC speeder, AT-RT, AT-TE, Venator-class Star Destroyer (home station). All flight and armor operation is qualification-gated.

### 6.4 Enemy Forces

Separatist Droid Army: B1 battle droids, B2 super battle droids, droidekas, BX commando droids, tactical droids, dwarf spider droids, AATs, vulture droids, Trade Federation and Techno Union assets.

Secondary antagonists available to event planners: pirates, mercenaries, planetary insurgents, rogue Republic elements.

## 8. Deviations Register

Deliberate departures from source material, with reasoning. **This section is the honest list.** Anyone who catches us contradicting canon should find it here.

| Deviation | Reasoning |

## 9. Ruling Process

When something isn't covered here:

1. **Ask in the lore channel.** Most questions are answered in a minute by someone who knows.
2. **If unresolved, file a Ruling Request** — a short post stating the question, the sources you've found, and what you'd like to do.
3. **The Lore Officer rules within 7 days.** Rulings are published in the rulings log.
4. **Rulings are binding and become part of this document** at the next revision.
5. **Management may overturn a Lore Officer ruling**, in writing, with reasoning published.

**Standing guidance for rulings:** when in doubt, favor the option that produces better roleplay for more players over the option that is more technically accurate. We are running a community, not a citation exercise.

## 10. The Attitude We Want

Lore knowledge is a way to make the world feel real. It is not a status game.

- **Correct people kindly and once.** "Hey, we're Phase II standard here — Phase I needs Sergeant and a write-up" is helpful. Six paragraphs with wiki links is not.
- **Do not lore-lawyer during operations.** If something's wrong, note it and raise it after.
- **New players get latitude.** Someone who thinks all clones are named Rex is a person we haven't taught yet.
- **The Lore Officer's job is to enable, not to gatekeep.** If someone has a cool idea that bends the setting slightly, the default answer is "let's find a way."
