# STAFF GUIDE
### [SERVER NAME] — Trial Moderator and above
*Role guide 4 of 4 · Drawn from the Staff Handbook and Charter*

---

## The job — all of it

1. **Keep players safe** — harassment, hate, predatory behavior
2. **Keep the game fair** — cheating, exploiting, griefing
3. **Keep the server running** — technical issues, stuck players, broken props

**That's the entire list.** Staff don't run the roleplay, don't resolve in-character disputes, don't command troops, and don't use tools to shape the story.

If you took this for the powers, it will show inside a month.

---

## The firewall — read this twice

> **Staff authority grants zero in-character authority. Command authority grants zero staff powers.**

- A Superadmin playing a CT takes orders from a Sergeant and says *"Yes, Sergeant."*
- A Marshal Commander cannot ban, kick, mute, or warn.
- You may hold both — **declare which hat you're wearing the moment it matters.**
- **You may never use staff tools in a dispute you are personally part of.** Call someone else. Every time. No exceptions.

**Violating the firewall is Strike-and-review on the first occurrence.** It's the fastest way off the team.

---

## Evidence — the hardest rule here

> **No evidence, no punishment. If the log has no evidence attached, the appeal is granted automatically.**

No exceptions. Not for obvious cases, not for repeat offenders, not for Management.

| Offense | Required |
|---|---|
| Chat | Screenshot with timestamp **and surrounding context** |
| Voice | Audio clip, or 2+ independent witness statements |
| Cheating | Video clip, 30 seconds minimum, showing the behavior |
| Exploiting | Video showing the exploit in use |
| RDM / griefing | Video or damage logs + witness statement |
| Harassment | Screenshots of the **pattern**, not one message |

**Context is mandatory.** One line without what preceded it is a fragment, not evidence. **Clips show the behavior, not the aftermath.** Collect witness statements separately — don't let them compare notes first.

**Evidence gets attached at the time of punishment**, not scrambled for later when someone appeals.

**Uncertain means no punishment.** An unjust warning damages that player's whole relationship with the server; a violation you let slide affects one incident. The asymmetry isn't close.

## Running a sit

1. **Acknowledge the report.** "On my way" or "Standby." Never leave one hanging.
2. **Gather evidence before you pull anyone** — don't tip off a suspected cheater by teleporting them.
3. Pull both parties.
4. **Reporter speaks first, uninterrupted.**
5. **Reported speaks, uninterrupted.**
6. Ask clarifying questions. Don't lecture.
7. **Rule** — state the rule, the finding, the punishment, plainly.
8. **Log it** — rule, evidence, punishment, reasoning.
9. Return both parties.

**Do:** stay calm regardless of tone · explain the rule, not just the penalty · tell them how to appeal · thank the reporter even when the report doesn't hold up.

**Don't:** argue · threaten extra punishment for arguing · discuss other players' punishments · make jokes at anyone's expense · rule on something you're unsure about.

### When you're being abused

You're not required to absorb it. Warn once — *"I'm ending this sit if this continues"* — then end it, apply the appropriate punishment for the conduct, log it, move on.

**You do not have to win the argument.** You are not required to make them agree with you.

---

## Escalate — don't guess

- The punishment exceeds your ban limit
- It involves another staff member, Head of Staff, or Management
- **You're personally involved**
- Cheating suspected but evidence is ambiguous
- Tier 4 or 5
- The rule is unclear or the situation isn't covered
- **You're angry**

That last one is real. *"I'm too heated for this, can someone take it"* is a completely acceptable sentence and nobody will think less of you.

---

## Recuse yourself when

You're a party to the incident · the player is in your battalion and it's battalion-related · they're a close friend or someone you have friction with · **you issued the punishment being appealed** · you stand to gain in character.

**Recusal is free and expected.** The cost of not recusing is that every ruling you ever make becomes suspect.

## The punishment matrix

| Tier | First | Second | Third |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** Minor | Verbal warning (logged) | Formal warning | 1–3 day ban |
| **2** Disruptive | Formal warning | 1–3 days | 7 days |
| **3** Serious | 3–7 days | 14–30 days | Permanent |
| **4** Severe | 30 days | Permanent | — |
| **5** Zero tolerance | Permanent | — | — |

**Correction before punishment.** A Tier 1 from a new member gets an explanation first — a rule nobody taught them is our failure, not their violation.

**Adjust one tier either way with written justification.** More than one needs Senior Admin approval.

**Non-ban options exist and are usually better:** voice mute, text mute, prop restriction, job restriction. Reach for these before a ban.

**Tier 1 and 2 warnings expire after 90 days clean.**

## Conduct

| |
|---|
| No tools for personal gain, entertainment, or IC advantage |
| No spawning items for yourself or others outside approved events |
| No noclip, godmode, or spectate outside an active sit |
| No discussing active cases outside the staff channel |
| No accepting anything of value connected to your duties |
| **Donation status is invisible in a sit** — preferential treatment is immediate removal |
| **No retaliation** against anyone who appealed or reported you — permanent bar |
| Hold yourself to the Charter more strictly than anyone else, publicly |

**Two strikes.** Strike one is documented and known to the staff team. Strike two is removal. Immediate removal for: firewall violation, punishing without evidence twice, retaliation, falsified evidence, Tier 4/5 conduct, or paid preferential treatment.

**Staff have the same appeal rights as players.** Removals can go to the Ombudsman.

---

## Take the LOA

**Free, unlimited, no justification required.** Post it in the staff channel and go. It doesn't count against activity or standing.

Burnout kills staff teams. Every single time. Take the LOA before you become the person snapping at players in sits.

---

## The standard

Every decision gets measured against one question:

> **Would this look defensible if it were posted publicly, with full context, tomorrow?**

Yes → act. No → don't. Unsure → escalate.

That's the whole job.
